# COMPETITOR_RESEARCH.md – Calgary Diner Benchmarks
## Project: John's Breakfast & Lunch (Calgary, AB)

### Executive Summary
Calgary's breakfast and brunch ecosystem combines legacy neighborhood diners (like *The Basic on Centre*, *Blue Star Diner*, *Red's Diner*) and modern aesthetic brunch hotspots (like *Brekkie Cafe*, *Maven*, *Egg & Spoon*). 

To ensure **John's Breakfast & Lunch** feels authentic to Calgary rather than generic, the website blends the warm, unpretentious hospitality of an authentic 2007 diner with the high-craft presentation of modern web standards.

---

### Calgary Benchmarks & Design Lessons

| Reference Restaurant | Location | Key Strengths & Observations | What to Apply to John's Breakfast & Lunch |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Basic on Centre** | 1115 Centre St NW | Closest business-style peer: family-owned, comfort breakfast, unpretentious, approachable. | Preserve genuine neighborhood warmth, avoid corporate sterile language, emphasize homemade feel. |
| **Brekkie Cafe** | 125-20 Westpark Link SW (`brekkie.ca`) | Large editorial food photography, rich hero visuals, warm cream branding, elegant serif typography. | High-impact breakfast hero section, signature dish spotlight cards, generous whitespace. |
| **Egg & Spoon** | 7729 Macleod Trail SW (`eggandspoon.ca`) | Clean navigation, straightforward menu categories, clear hours, seamless mobile experience. | Intuitive menu tab switching, immediate "Get Directions" & click-to-call affordances. |
| **Maven Brunch** | 1006 17 Ave SW (`mavenbrunch.ca`) | Emotional storytelling, botanical warm aesthetics, photography hierarchy. | Deepen the "Our Story" narrative honoring founders John & Wendy Ly since 2007. |
| **Blue Star Diner** | 809 1 Ave NE (Bridgeland) | Strong community identity, local pride, comfort food positioning. | Highlight the Mount Pleasant / NW Calgary community connection and customer love. |
| **Red's Diner** | 286 10 St NW (Kensington) | Classic diner identity, structured menu categorization, traditional breakfast focus. | Structured categorization: Breakfast, Bennies, Omelettes, Pancakes, Lunch, Asian-Inspired. |

### Anti-Slop Differentiation
- **Anti-AI tells:** No neon gradients, no generic floating glass cards, no stock photos of unrelated European bistros.
- **Calgary Diner Authenticity:** Warm cream canvas (`#FFF8ED`), deep roasted espresso (`#35251F`), classic diner tomato red (`#B94432`), and Canadian breakfast classics (Breakfast Poutine with curds & gravy, Eggs Benedict, Chorizo Omelette).
